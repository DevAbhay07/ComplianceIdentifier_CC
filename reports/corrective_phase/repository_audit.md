## 1. Executive Summary of Audit Findings

This audit systematically answers all 20 foundational questions regarding the current repository state, dataset provenance, feature pipelines, model targets, leakage boundaries, and execution paths.

## 2. Answers to the 20 Audit Questions

### Q1: What currently extracts declarations?
- **Current State**: `src/inference/evidence_parser.py` parses raw OCR text and metadata strings into regex-based declaration matches (`D01` to `D07`).
- **Correction Required**: The previous parser extracted only boolean presence flags without structured value normalization (e.g. quantity magnitudes, units, dates, contact entities). The corrected `src/extraction/declaration_extractor.py` preserves both raw snippets and normalized values.

### Q2: What currently extracts actual values?
- **Current State**: Previously, regex groups matched raw substrings, but these were largely discarded or reduced to presence booleans during feature vector generation.
- **Correction Required**: `src/extraction/declaration_extractor.py` and `declaration_schema.py` explicitly extract and retain actual numerical quantities ($250$), units (`g`, `ml`), date components (month $2$, year $2026$), prices ($₹1063.00$), tax inclusive flags (`True`), emails, and phone numbers.

### Q3: What currently creates D01-D07?
- **Current State**: In Phase 8/9, frozen multi-label models (`models/phase_8/`) predicted declaration states. In Phase 11/12, `parse_product_evidence` constructed internal evidence dictionaries.
- **Correction Required**: Standardize on a single, unified declaration evidence object across all pipeline steps via `src/extraction/declaration_schema.py`.

### Q4: What currently predicts product compliance?
- **Current State**: The top-level verdict was generated by the `EvidenceAwareComplianceDecisionEngine` (`src/decision/product_compliance_decision.py`), while secondary direct ML models (`RandomForest_SET_D`) provided diagnostic softmax probabilities.
- **Correction Required**: Maintain the primary/secondary separation clearly. Train product-level models strictly on declaration-centric feature representations (`Set A` through `Set E`) and evaluate whether ML or rule-assisted decision is scientifically supportable.

### Q5: Does the current ML model consume declaration features?
- **Current State**: In Phase 11, `Phase11FeatureExtractor` transformed raw text + 29 YOLO visual features into a $D=741$ vector. The direct ML model was primarily relying on text length, TF-IDF, and visual bounding box distributions, rather than explicit semantic declaration features (quantities, dates, prices).
- **Correction Required**: The corrected `src/features/declaration_feature_extractor.py` explicitly constructs feature vectors from structured D01–D07 evidence, numerical values, date parsability, OCR confidence, and cross-image fusion.

### Q6: Does the current final verdict come from ML, rules, or both?
- **Current State**: The primary verdict in Phase 10–12 came from the **Statutory Decision Engine** (Rule 6 logic), while the direct ML model ran in parallel as a secondary diagnostic.
- **Correction Required**: Maintain this distinction with total transparency: pure ML models and rule/hybrid engines must be evaluated and reported separately without conflating their metrics.

### Q7: How are YOLO outputs currently used?
- **Current State**: YOLOv8 (`models/phase_11/yolov8_packaging.pt`) detects 9 packaging visual classes and extracts 29 continuous spatial/confidence features (`yolo_feature_extractor.py`).
- **Correction Required**: YOLO must be treated strictly as **packaging visual localization assistance** (identifying ROI coordinates for OCR cropping). YOLO presence/absence is supplementary visual evidence; YOLO=0 must NEVER equal legal absence.

### Q8: How are OCR outputs currently used?
- **Current State**: OCR/Vision text was fed into `parse_product_evidence` regexes and concatenated into description fields for feature transformers.
- **Correction Required**: OCR/Vision is the **primary source of semantic declaration truth** (answering *what* the package actually says).

### Q9: How are multiple images currently handled?
- **Current State**: Single image was processed at a time; no cross-image evidence aggregation or conflict resolution existed.
- **Correction Required**: Implement `src/extraction/evidence_fusion.py` where multiple packaging views (front, back, sides) of a single product are fused into ONE unified D01–D07 product representation before feature extraction and product-level ML prediction.

### Q10: Which datasets contain product-level compliance labels?
- `data/processed/pilot_ground_truth_75_v1.jsonl`: 75 products (human verified D01–D07 declarations; derived product compliance).
- `data/processed/flipkart_weak_labels_v1.jsonl`: 1,663 products (rule-derived weak silver labels).
- `real_product_validation/manual_reference/`: 20 real products (human verified packaging ground truth).

### Q11: Which datasets contain only object/declaration-region labels?
- `data/external/yolo_candidate_dataset`: 249 packaging images, 482 bounding boxes across 9 visual classes (`brand`, `mrp details`, `weight`, `address`, `consumer`, `origin`, `details`, `tax`, `barcode`). Contains **zero** product-level compliance labels.

### Q12: Which datasets are weak/silver?
- `data/processed/flipkart_weak_labels_v1.jsonl` (1,663 products): Automatically generated weak silver labels derived from Flipkart catalog metadata via Phase 6 heuristics.

### Q13: Which datasets are gold/human verified?
- `data/processed/pilot_ground_truth_75_v1.jsonl` (75 products, catalog domain, SHA-256 locked `37dc62a3...`).
- `real_product_validation/` (20 products, physical packaging domain, evaluation benchmark).

### Q14: Are any existing labels derived from D01-D07?
- Yes. Product-level compliance targets (`COMPLIANT` vs `NON_COMPLIANT`) in the weak dataset and benchmark references are derived from the conjunction of mandatory declarations: if any mandatory declaration (`D01`–`D06`) is `ABSENT`, target is `NON_COMPLIANT`.

### Q15: Are any existing features derived from the final target?
- In historical feature sets (Phase 7/8/11), features were extracted from raw text, specifications, and YOLO boxes, with strict leakage audits passing. However, to prevent subtle circularity, target-derived status labels must never be directly fed as raw features into ML models predicting that exact target.

### Q16: Is there target leakage?
- No direct target column was included in feature matrices. The corrective phase adds `src/evaluation/leakage_audit.py` to enforce that neither the ground-truth target, rule-generated verdict, nor test-fitted scalers exist in the training feature pipelines.

### Q17: Is the existing 20-product benchmark used anywhere in training?
- **No.** The 20 real products in `real_product_validation/` are strictly isolated as an evaluation benchmark ($N=20$). They are never used for model training, threshold tuning, or feature selection.

### Q18: Is the 75-product human reference used anywhere in training?
- **No.** The 75-product human reference dataset is frozen and immutable with verified SHA-256 hash `37dc62a308a67899ee40d782b2163d56b6c472edc47b6f56059048649b877520`.

### Q19: Does the existing Streamlit app bypass the intended pipeline?
- The Streamlit app (`app/streamlit_app.py`) calls `app/utils/inference_bridge.py`. In Phase 12, the bridge wrapped the multimodal pipeline. In this corrective phase, the bridge is updated to route exclusively through `src/inference/declaration_centric_pipeline.py`.

### Q20: Which existing modules can safely be reused?
- `src/vision/yolo_detector.py` (YOLO packaging localization).
- `src/inference/vision_ocr.py` & `mock_vision_ocr.py` (Gemini & Mock providers).
- `models/phase_11/yolov8_packaging.pt` (frozen YOLO weights).
- `data/external/yolo_candidate_dataset` (packaging spatial annotations).
- `real_product_validation/images/` (20 benchmark images).
- `data/processed/pilot_ground_truth_75_v1.jsonl` (frozen reference).

## 3. Audit Conclusion & Roadmap

The repository is well-structured and historical phases (Phases 8, 9, 10, 11, 12) remain intact and frozen. The corrective phase builds isolated declaration-centric modules (`src/extraction/`, `src/features/`, `src/models/`, `src/inference/`) fulfilling all 56 specification mandates.
