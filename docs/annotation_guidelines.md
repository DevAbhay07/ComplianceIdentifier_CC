# Annotation Guidelines — NitiMark Compliance Dataset

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines define how human annotators should review product records and assign compliance labels for the NitiMark ML training dataset.

The goal is to create **ground-truth compliance annotations** that are:
- Based on the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
- Applied to real product records from the Flipkart dataset
- Reviewed by humans (not generated by automated rules)
- Scientifically consistent and reproducible

---

## 2. What the Annotator Receives

For each product to be annotated, the annotator will receive:

| Input | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Product name | Flipkart listing | Title of the product |
| Product category | Flipkart listing | Category tree |
| Brand | Flipkart listing | May be missing |
| Description | Flipkart listing | Rich text product description |
| Product specifications | Flipkart listing | Key-value pairs |
| Retail price | Flipkart listing | Listed MRP / discounted price |
| Product image(s) | Flipkart listing / downloaded | If available |
| Applicable legal requirements | `configs/legal_requirements.yaml` | Based on product category |

---

## 3. Applicable Legal Requirements

The annotator should check whether the product listing **contains evidence of** the following mandatory declarations, as required by the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011:

| Declaration ID | Declaration | Rule Reference |
|---|---|---|
| D01 | Name and address of manufacturer/packer/importer | Rule 6(1)(a), Rule 10 |
| D02 | Common/generic name of commodity | Rule 6(1)(b) |
| D03 | Net quantity (weight, volume, or number) | Rule 6(1)(c), Rules 11-13 |
| D04 | Month and year of manufacture/packing/import | Rule 6(1)(d) |
| D05 | Maximum Retail Price (MRP), inclusive of all taxes | Rule 6(1)(e) |
| D06 | Consumer care contact details | Rule 6(2) |
| D07 | Country of origin (for imported products) | Rule 6(1)(a) |

**Important**: Not all declarations can be verified from an e-commerce listing alone. The annotator should record what is observable, not guess.

---

## 4. Labeling Schema

### 4.1 Per-Declaration Labels

For each declaration, the annotator assigns one of:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `PRESENT` | The information is clearly present in the listing (metadata, description, specs, or image) |
| `ABSENT` | The information is not present anywhere in the available data |
| `PARTIAL` | Some relevant information exists but is incomplete (e.g., brand name but no address) |
| `NOT_APPLICABLE` | The declaration does not apply to this product category |
| `CANNOT_DETERMINE` | Cannot determine presence/absence from available information |

### 4.2 Overall Compliance Label

Based on the per-declaration assessments, the annotator assigns one overall label:

| Label | Criteria |
|---|---|
| `COMPLIANT` | All applicable mandatory declarations are PRESENT |
| `NON_COMPLIANT` | One or more applicable mandatory declarations are ABSENT |
| `UNCERTAIN` | One or more declarations are PARTIAL or CANNOT_DETERMINE, and no declaration is clearly ABSENT |

**Rule**: Do NOT force uncertain cases into COMPLIANT or NON_COMPLIANT. Honest uncertainty is better than fabricated certainty.

---

## 5. Annotation Record Format

Each annotated product should produce a record like:

```yaml
product_id: "abc123"
product_name: "Brand X Face Cream 50ml"
category: "Beauty and Personal Care"
annotator_id: "annotator_01"
annotation_date: "2026-09-01"

declarations:
  D01_manufacturer_name_address:
    label: "PARTIAL"
    evidence: "Brand name 'Brand X' found in listing, but no manufacturer address"
    source: "brand field, product_specifications"

  D02_commodity_name:
    label: "PRESENT"
    evidence: "Product clearly identified as 'Face Cream'"
    source: "product_name"

  D03_net_quantity:
    label: "PRESENT"
    evidence: "'50ml' mentioned in product name and specifications"
    source: "product_name, product_specifications"

  D04_manufacture_date:
    label: "CANNOT_DETERMINE"
    evidence: "No date information in listing"
    source: "none"

  D05_mrp:
    label: "PRESENT"
    evidence: "Retail price Rs. 450 listed"
    source: "retail_price"

  D06_consumer_care:
    label: "ABSENT"
    evidence: "No customer care contact found in listing"
    source: "none"

  D07_country_of_origin:
    label: "NOT_APPLICABLE"
    evidence: "Product appears to be domestically manufactured"
    source: "product_specifications"

overall_label: "UNCERTAIN"
overall_reason: "D01 is only partial (brand but no address), D04 and D06 cannot be verified from listing data"
confidence: "MEDIUM"
```

---

## 6. Important Annotation Rules

1. **Annotate what you can observe**, not what you assume.
2. **Do not invent information** that is not in the product record.
3. **Use CANNOT_DETERMINE** when the listing does not provide enough information to make a judgment.
4. **Record your evidence** — cite which field(s) you found the information in.
5. **Be consistent** — apply the same criteria to similar products.
6. **Distinguish between listing data and packaging data** — if you see information in a product image that is not in the listing metadata, note the source as "product_image".
7. **D04 (manufacture date) will almost always be CANNOT_DETERMINE** from listing data alone — this is expected and correct.
8. **D06 (consumer care) will often be ABSENT** from listings — this is expected and correct.
9. **For imported products**, D07 (country of origin) becomes critical. Check product specifications for "Country of Origin" or "Imported by" fields.

## 7. Product Categories to Prioritize

Based on the Flipkart dataset analysis, the following categories are most relevant for annotation:

### Clearly Applicable (Packaged Commodities)
- Beauty and Personal Care (710 products)
- Kitchen & Dining (647 products)
- Baby Care (483 products)
- Tools & Hardware (391 products)
- Home Improvement (81 products)

### Partially Applicable (May Contain Packaged Commodities)
- Automotive (1,012 products) — car care products, lubricants
- Home Decor & Festive Needs (929 products) — candles, sprays
- Pens & Stationery (313 products) — packaged pens, ink

### Recommended Starting Subset
Start with **Beauty and Personal Care** and **Baby Care** — these categories most clearly fall under the PC Rules and contain products with packaging labels (creams, shampoos, diapers, baby food, etc.).

---

## 8. Known Limitations

- **Images may not be available**: The Flipkart CDN URLs are from 2016 and likely broken. Initial annotations may need to be based on listing metadata only.
- **Some declarations cannot be verified from listings**: D04 (manufacture date) and D06 (consumer care) are rarely present in e-commerce listings. This is a fundamental limitation of using listing data.
- **Brand ≠ Manufacturer**: The brand field in the Flipkart dataset is not the same as the manufacturer name and address required by law.
- **MRP in listing ≠ MRP on package**: The price shown on Flipkart may be the discounted price, not the MRP printed on the package.
